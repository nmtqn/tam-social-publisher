Information is used only to provide Pinterest publishing and content management functionality.

Tam Social Publisher uses Pinterest OAuth authentication. The application does not collect Pinterest passwords or Pinterest session cookies.

Data Sharing

Users may revoke access to the application through their Pinterest account settings.
